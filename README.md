# India's Groundwater Crisis Dashboard (2015–2022)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing India's groundwater depletion using the **Atal Jal** dataset, tracking water levels across states, districts, and seasons from 2015 to 2022.

## 📊 Overview

Groundwater is a critical resource for India's agriculture and drinking water supply, but decades of over-extraction have pushed many regions into crisis. This dashboard turns raw Atal Jal monitoring data into a clear, interactive story — surfacing where the crisis is worst, how it's trending over time, and what can be done about it.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **80.64%** of monitored wells are now classified as critical.
- **Gujarat** is the most affected state, with an average water level of **46.97 m**.
- **Maharashtra** is the healthiest state, with an average water level of **8.50 m**.
- The sharpest decline occurred between **2020–2022**, in the **post-monsoon** period.

## 📄 Dashboard Pages

**Page 1 — Overview**
- KPI cards: Avg. Water Level, Total Wells, Water Level (2015), Water Level (2022), Critical Wells %
- State-wise filled map of average water levels
- Year-over-year trend line, broken down by season
- Slicers: season, state

**Page 2 — Regional Breakdown**
- Donut chart: water level distribution by season
- Bar chart: average water level by district
- Clustered column chart: water level by season and state
- Slicers: state, year, season

**Page 3 — Trends & Recommendations**
- Waterfall chart: year-over-year change in water levels
- Treemap: state/district water levels
- Key findings and policy recommendations

## ✅ Recommendations

- Promote rainwater harvesting
- Increase afforestation in high-risk states
- Prioritize critical wells first for intervention
- Build community awareness
